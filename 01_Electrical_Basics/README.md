## Learning Objectives

After completing this section, I should be able to:

- Explain what electric charge and current represent.
- Understand voltage as electric potential difference.
- Explain the physical meaning of resistance.
- Apply Ohm's Law to basic circuits.
- Calculate electrical power and energy.
- Relate voltage, current and power to real electrical systems.
- Understand the relevance of these quantities to power electronics.

## Key Equations

### Current

$$
I = \frac{dQ}{dt}
$$

### Voltage

$$
V = \frac{W}{Q}
$$

### Ohm's Law

$$
V = IR
$$

### Electrical Power

$$
P = VI
$$

For a resistor:

$$
P = I^2R = \frac{V^2}{R}
$$

### Electrical Energy

$$
E = Pt
$$
